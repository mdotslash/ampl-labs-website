The Opportunity

Shape The Future of Digital Cultural Heritage

Join AMPL Labs for a digital cultural heritage professional development intensive for artists interested in working with communities and cultural institutions.

The intensive will focus on using accessible 3D scanning technology and open-source digital collections to create virtual museums, apps and media for preserving and protecting cultural knowledge. We'll also explore how emergent technologies can be used for innovative cultural collaboration with vulnerable communities.

Designed and Led by Working Artists

This training is offered by Mayowa Tomori (Award-Winning Nigerian-American artist), with additional content provided by Claudia Miranda (Mexican-American 3D developer), Olivia Cueva (design technologist) and Monica Silva Lovato (Indigenous creative technologist from San Felipe / Santo Domingo).

Together, they will talk about how they use emerging technology to empower cultural researchers in vulnerable communities and decolonize cultural institutions.

Participants will also learn about:


  • 3D Scanning: Step-by-step hands-on training on creating high-fidelity 3D models for archives.
  • Virtual Museum Technology: Tips for integrating 3D content and metadata into databases and archives using Smithsonian Voyager, Murkutu CMS and other open-source tools.
  • Activating Collections: Creative tips for artists and researchers looking to adapt and activate archival content in their practice.
  • Ethics & Collaborating with Institutions: Practical guides for minimizing harm, taking care of your team, and supporting vulnerable communites while working with sensitive collections.
  • Funding Strategy: How to build sustainable business partnerships with cultural institutions, and fund collaborative research using public and private sources.
The Training

Workshop Curriculum

The intensive will give you the technical skills for leading 3D and XR archival research and creative projects in cultural institutions. We can also adapt the curriculum to fit your unique use-case or project.

01: Foundation

Digitizating Objects in 3D

This module covers use-cases for 3D/AR in cultural institutions and includes a hands-on photogrammetry intensive with a partner cultural institution or private archivist.

02: Implementation

Digital Publishing

Learn how to create interactive, web-based 3D & AR experiences that are mobile-friendly and accessible. We also show you how to integrate 3D content into games, apps and New Media applications.

03: Expression & Exploration

Collaborative Worldbuilding

Explore how that 3D spaces can be used for Community Collaboration and self-expression.

04: Innovation

Speculative Digital Futures

Dream up emergent, decolonial and restorative uses for digital cultural heritage and archives.

Case Study

Casting Memories:
Smithsonian x AMPL Labs

In 2022, AMPL Labs led an award-winning collaboration with the Smithsonian Institution and Mozilla Foundation to animate their digital collections using new forms of storytelling and emerging technology.

Cultural Technology for The People

We defined an accessible 3D-scanning, archiving methodology that moves away from extractive tech and toward restorative infrastructure.

Decolonizing the Archive

We led a reparative process that invited artists, technologists from impacted communities to participate in the co-design, co-creation, or destruction of archives held by cultural institutions.

Innovative Possibilities (I.P.)

We developed new models for ownership, IP and less-exploitative licensing to make it easier for cultural institutions to work with Nigerian artists, program designers, researchers and communities.
